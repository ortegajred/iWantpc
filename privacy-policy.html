1. About this Privacy Policy

iWant PC ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and disclose personal information when you visit or use the iWant PC website, create an account, purchase computer components or accessories, make a payment, or request a service, PC build consultation, repair, diagnostic check, or warranty claim.

We handle personal information in accordance with applicable Philippine privacy laws, including Republic Act No. 10173, or the Data Privacy Act of 2012, and its implementing rules and regulations.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: your name, email address, phone number, and information needed to create and maintain your customer account.
  • Order and delivery information: items purchased, quantities, order history, delivery address, delivery instructions, and order status.
  • Payment information: GCash transaction reference numbers and payment-proof images that you voluntarily upload for payment verification. Do not provide your GCash MPIN, password, or one-time PIN (OTP).
  • Service and warranty information: device or component details, issue descriptions, preferred visit dates, purchase or receipt references, and information needed to process your request.
  • Messages and support information: information you provide when you contact us or communicate with our team.

We collect information that you submit through forms and account features, as well as records created when we process your orders, payments, and requests.

3. How we use your information

We use personal information only for legitimate and disclosed business purposes, including to:

  • create, verify, and manage customer accounts;
  • process orders, payments, delivery arrangements, and order updates;
  • verify GCash payments using the transaction reference and proof you submit;
  • manage service bookings, PC build consultations, repair requests, diagnostics, and warranty claims;
  • contact you about your account, order, payment, delivery, or service request;
  • provide customer support and resolve disputes;
  • protect the website, prevent fraud or misuse, and maintain accurate business records; and
  • comply with applicable legal, regulatory, accounting, and record-keeping requirements.

4. Payments and uploaded proof

When you pay through GCash, you may be asked to enter your transaction reference number and upload an image of your payment confirmation. This information is used to help verify the payment associated with your order.

Uploaded payment-proof images are restricted to authorized staff accounts that need access for payment-verification work. They are not made available to other customers. Please never upload an image that exposes your MPIN, password, OTP, or other login credentials.

Payment processing may also be subject to GCash's own terms and privacy practices. You should review the relevant provider's notices when using its services.

5. When we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share only the information reasonably needed with:

  • authorized iWant PC personnel who handle accounts, orders, payment verification, deliveries, customer support, or service and warranty requests;
  • technology providers that support the website, including Supabase services used for authentication, database, and storage functions;
  • payment, delivery, logistics, repair, or warranty partners when sharing is necessary to complete your transaction or request; and
  • government authorities, regulators, courts, or other parties when disclosure is required or permitted by law.

Where a service provider processes information for us, it should be given access only as needed to perform its service and subject to appropriate safeguards and applicable requirements.

6. Storage, retention, and security

We use reasonable organizational, physical, and technical safeguards intended to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, disclosure, or misuse. Access to information is limited according to work responsibilities.

We retain personal information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, to complete transactions and support requests, to maintain required business records, to resolve disputes, or to comply with legal obligations. Information that is no longer needed will be securely deleted, anonymized, or disposed of when appropriate and legally permitted.

No website, storage system, or electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please protect your password and notify us if you believe your account has been accessed without permission.

7. Your privacy rights

Subject to the conditions and limitations under applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • be informed about how your personal information is collected and processed;
  • request access to your personal information;
  • request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • object to or withdraw consent to certain processing, where applicable;
  • request blocking, erasure, or removal when the legal requirements are met;
  • request data portability where applicable;
  • seek compensation for damages caused by unlawful or inaccurate processing, as provided by law; and
  • file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission when you believe your privacy rights have been violated.

To protect your account, we may need to verify your identity before acting on a privacy request. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

For more information, visit the National Privacy Commission's guide to data subject rights.

8. Cookies and website technologies

The website and its service providers may use essential browser storage, session technologies, or similar tools to support account sign-in, maintain website functions, protect sessions, and improve reliability. You can manage cookies and site data through your browser settings, although disabling essential technologies may affect some website features.

9. Links to other services

The website may link to third-party services, such as payment or delivery providers. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of websites or services that we do not control.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes will be communicated through the website or another reasonable channel when appropriate.
